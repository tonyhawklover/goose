# Goose Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Goose doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data.

## What stays on your phone

Everything Goose stores stays on your device:

- **Profiles:** the names and icons you choose, and the apps and categories each one blocks.
- **Tags and QR codes:** the codes Goose writes to your NFC tags or puts in your QR codes, and which profile each belongs to.
- **Block history:** when each block started and ended.
- **Schedules and daily limits:** their times and settings.

None of this is sent anywhere. Goose has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't use the network at all.

## Screen Time

Goose uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block apps. Apple never tells Goose which apps or websites you picked. It gives Goose opaque tokens it can use to block them, and nothing else. Goose can't see your Screen Time usage data. For daily limits, iOS only tells Goose when a limit has been reached, not how you spent the time.

## NFC and camera

Goose only uses NFC when you start a scan or write a tag, and only reads the code stored on the tag. It only uses the camera while you're scanning a QR code. Nothing from the camera is saved or sent anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Goose deletes all of its data from your phone and removes any active blocks.

## Children

Goose doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
