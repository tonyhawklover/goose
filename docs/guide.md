# How to Use Goose

## Getting started

1. Tap **What's Blocked** at the bottom of the main screen and choose the apps, categories and websites to block.
2. In the same screen, choose how you want to unlock.
3. Tap the lock button at the top of the main screen.

## Unlock methods

Each profile has its own unlock method.

- **Tag or QR:** lock and unlock by scanning an NFC tag or a QR code. Keep it somewhere you'd have to get up to reach. This is the most effective option.
- **Timer:** block for a set time, from 15 minutes to 8 hours. You can't unlock early, and apps unblock on their own when time is up, even if Goose is closed.
- **Button:** lock and unlock with a tap. A gentle reminder rather than a hard block.

## Tags and QR codes

- Tap **+** on the main screen to write an NFC tag or make a QR code.
- Any tag or QR code works on any profile. Give a profile a **dedicated** one and only that one can lock or unlock it. Dedicated profiles show a key.
- **NFC tags:** any rewritable tag an iPhone can read works, like NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 stickers and cards. MIFARE Classic tags, common in cheap key fobs, don't work with iPhone. [Search for NTAG213 stickers](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=shop&q=NTAG213+NFC+stickers) (a plain search, not an affiliate link).
- **QR codes:** print it, or keep it on another device. A phone can't scan a code on its own screen. You can view or reprint it any time from **+** → **Manage Tags and QR Codes**.
- Lost your tag while blocked? You can still make a new one with **+**.

## Profiles

- Tap **Add another profile** to make one, for example Work, Evenings or Weekends.
- With more than one profile, tap a profile to switch to it, and touch and hold it to edit.

## Widgets and the Action Button

These open Goose and use the profile's unlock method. They never skip a tag or end a timer early.

- **Home Screen widget:** touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and choose Goose.
- **Lock Screen widget:** touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, then tap the area under the clock and choose Goose.
- **Action Button** (iPhone 15 Pro and later): open **Settings**, then **Action Button**, swipe to **Controls**, and choose Goose.
- **Control Center:** open Control Center, touch and hold an empty space, tap **Add a Control** and choose Goose.

## Schedules and daily limits

Find these under the **menu button** (☰, top left).

- **Schedules** lock a profile automatically, for example every night from 9 PM to 7 AM or during work hours on weekdays, and unlock it when they end. They work even when Goose is closed. You can still unlock early the profile's usual way: scan its tag or QR code, or tap for Button profiles. Timer profiles stay locked until the schedule ends.
- **Daily limits** let you use a profile's apps for a set time each day, like 30 minutes in total. Once you reach it, Goose locks until midnight, just like pressing the lock button. Unlocking the profile's usual way turns the limit off for the rest of the day. Timer profiles confirm instead, since timers can't normally end early.
- **Stats** shows your last 7 days, today's total, your streak and your longest block.

## Quotes

- Tap the **menu button** (☰, top left), then **Quotes**, to turn quotes off or add your own.
- Turn off **Include Goose's quotes** to only see the ones you added.

## Good to know

- Goose is a commitment device, not an unbreakable lock. Deleting Goose or turning off its Screen Time access in Settings removes all blocks.
- NFC tags store a plain code, so someone with an NFC app could copy one.
- Goose is free, with no ads, no tracking, and no affiliate links.
